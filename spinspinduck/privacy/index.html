Spin Spin Duck! Privacy Policy

Spin Spin Duck! is an Android game from IFGAMES. For privacy questions or deletion requests, email tocher99@gmail.com. This policy applies to version 1.0, build 2, dated November 21, 2026.

Playing and saving

You can play without an account. The game stores your progress, collection, currency and settings on your device. Settings > Reset Progress clears your game progress. Clearing app storage also removes local settings.

Install and session measurement

IFGAMES uses AppsFlyer to measure installs and launches, understand which campaigns bring players to the game and detect attribution fraud. The SDK can send an app installation identifier, the Android advertising ID when available, install referrer, session and launch times, app version, device model and operating system, and diagnostic or performance signals. AppsFlyer receives the IP address with a request and can estimate the city or region. The game does not request GPS location. AppsFlyer processes measurement data for IFGAMES. Attribution reports may go to campaign partners when partner integrations are enabled.

Support and choices

If you email support, IFGAMES receives your email address and whatever you choose to include. This information is used to respond to your request. You can manage or delete the advertising ID in Android settings. This does not erase earlier measurement records.

Retention and deletion

Local progress is kept until you reset it or clear app storage. Attribution and diagnostic records are kept under the retention settings and limits applicable to the AppsFlyer account. Support messages are kept for as long as needed to handle the request and any applicable legal obligations. To request access or deletion, email the contact above with the game name and the approximate date of your install. We may ask for information to locate the relevant record. Records that can no longer be linked to your device may be impossible to identify.

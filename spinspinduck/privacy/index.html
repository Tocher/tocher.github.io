Spin Spin Duck! Privacy Policy

Spin Spin Duck! (package com.ifgames.spincycle) is an Android game from IFGAMES. For privacy questions or deletion requests, email tocher99@gmail.com. This policy is effective November 27, 2026.

In short: the game has no account and does not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos, precise location or payment details. To measure installs, advertising campaigns and how the game is played, it uses four third-party services: AppsFlyer, Meta (Facebook), GameAnalytics and AppMetrica. They receive device and usage data, including the Android advertising ID when available. IFGAMES does not sell personal data.

Playing and saving

You can play without an account. The game stores your progress, collection, currency and settings on your device. Settings > Reset Progress clears your game progress. Clearing app storage also removes local settings. Your saved game is not sent to any service.

Third-party services

Each service below collects data automatically while the game runs, over encrypted connections, and processes it under its own privacy policy. The services receive the IP address with each request and may use it to estimate the country, city or region. The game does not request GPS or other precise location, and location tracking is turned off in AppMetrica.

  • AppsFlyer measures installs and launches, shows which campaigns bring players to the game and detects attribution fraud. It can receive an app installation identifier, the advertising ID, install referrer, session and launch times, app version, device model and operating system, and diagnostic or performance signals. Attribution reports may go to campaign partners when partner integrations are enabled. AppsFlyer privacy policy
  • Meta (Facebook) SDK measures installs and app activations for advertising on Facebook and Instagram. It receives the advertising ID, device and app information, install referrer and automatically logged app events such as app launches. The game has no Facebook login and does not access your Facebook account. Meta privacy policy
  • GameAnalytics provides gameplay analytics. It receives a randomly generated user ID, device model, operating system, app version, country, session start and length, and gameplay events such as stage progress, upgrades bought, ducks collected and time spent on screens. GameAnalytics privacy policy
  • AppMetrica (Yandex) provides app analytics and crash reports. It receives device and app identifiers including the advertising ID, device model, operating system, app version, sessions, the same gameplay events and crash diagnostics. AppMetrica terms and Yandex privacy policy

IFGAMES uses this data in aggregated form to understand how many people install and play the game, where they stop playing, how stable the game is and how well advertising works. Our publishing partner may view the same aggregated statistics in these services to evaluate the game.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state, to send the data described above.
  • Advertising ID, Google Play Install Referrer and Android ad services, added by the AppsFlyer, Meta and AppMetrica SDKs, to measure where installs come from.
  • Vibration, for haptic feedback. It can be turned off in the game settings.

Support and choices

If you email support, IFGAMES receives your email address and whatever you choose to include. This information is used to respond to your request. You can reset or delete the advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, in Android settings. This does not erase earlier records. Uninstalling the game stops all data collection by it.

Retention and deletion

Local progress is kept until you reset it or clear app storage. Analytics, attribution and diagnostic records are kept by the services above under their retention settings and policies. IFGAMES does not keep a separate copy on its own servers. Support messages are kept for as long as needed to handle the request and any applicable legal obligations. To request access or deletion, email the contact above with the game name, the approximate date of your install and, if possible, your device's advertising ID. We will forward the request to the services listed above. Records that can no longer be linked to your device may be impossible to identify.

Children

Spin Spin Duck! is not directed at children under 13, and IFGAMES does not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe data about a child was collected, contact us and we will request its deletion.

Changes

If the game starts handling data differently, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.
